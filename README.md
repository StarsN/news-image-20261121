One image summing up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026: Russia's
biggest strike of the war on Ukraine, with missiles landing in Poland; an AI
sell-off dragging the S&P 500 and Nasdaq into correction; the Hague court
finding Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya; COP31 deadlocked
over fossil fuels; the UK and EU signing their biggest post-Brexit deals; a
pancreatic-cancer pill making tumours undetectable in one in twelve patients;
Gen Z protests across 25 European countries; and more. Every story on the
page was first reported on that day.

The image is drawn from scratch by `compose.py` (Pillow, no network, no
downloaded assets) and copied to the output directory by `main.py`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

To redraw the front page:

```sh
python3 compose.py
```
